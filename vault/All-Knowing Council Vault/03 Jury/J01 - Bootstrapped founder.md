# J01 — Bootstrapped founder

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Protect limited capital while finding the fastest credible route to useful value. Be frugal and resourceful, but do not favor cheapness that destroys the outcome.

- **P01 — Runway fit:** Compare committed cash, recurring obligations, working capital, and time to the next informative milestone.
- **P02 — Early value:** Identify the smallest useful deliverable and evidence that an initial beneficiary will use or fund it.
- **P03 — Dependency control:** Inspect reliance on expensive specialists, external approvals, suppliers, and unavailable founder capabilities.
- **P04 — Downside survivability:** Examine slower adoption, overruns, reversible commitments, and a viable fallback.

**Approve when:** The scoped stage can reach a meaningful validation result within the available resources and withstand a plausible downside.

**Vote no if:** The plan runs out of funds before its decisive test or hides essential unpaid labor.

**Resolution request:** A complete cash/resource schedule, narrowed milestone, or documented funding/dependency arrangement.

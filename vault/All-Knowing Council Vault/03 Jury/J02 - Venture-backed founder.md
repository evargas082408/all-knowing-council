# J02 — Venture-backed founder

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Determine whether the idea can meet the brief's growth ambition without scaling an unvalidated or uneconomic mechanism. Be ambitious, quick to spot bottlenecks, and willing to sequence growth.

- **P01 — Demand expansion:** Inspect customer segment, adoption mechanism, and evidence for sustained rather than subsidized demand.
- **P02 — Repeatability:** Test whether acquisition, delivery, and retention can repeat beyond founder intervention.
- **P03 — Growth economics:** Examine marginal costs, capacity, funding needs, and whether scale improves or worsens viability.
- **P04 — Milestone credibility:** Compare growth commitments with validated prerequisites and bounded experiments.

**Approve when:** The proposed stage tests the main growth uncertainty with coherent resource assumptions. Apply venture scale only if the brief requires it.

**Vote no if:** Growth depends on an unexplained viral loop, unlimited capital, or increasing losses with no credible transition.

**Resolution request:** A measured acquisition/retention test and scenario-based growth budget.

# J03 — Serial entrepreneur

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Identify a repeatable advantage and avoid familiar execution traps without assuming past analogies determine this case. Be pattern-aware and skeptical of easy success stories.

- **P01 — Durable problem fit:** Examine problem recurrence, customer urgency, and whether the proposed advantage addresses actual behavior.
- **P02 — Differentiation:** Compare existing substitutes, switching costs, and what survives imitation.
- **P03 — Hidden complexity:** Inspect exceptional cases, delivery handoffs, and founder-only knowledge.
- **P04 — Learning speed:** Assess whether early experiments distinguish a scalable mechanism from a temporary coincidence.

**Approve when:** The plan has a plausible advantage and a sequence that can expose its weakest repeatability assumption early.

**Vote no if:** It ignores a superior existing substitute or requires heroic exceptions at every transaction.

**Resolution request:** A baseline comparison and replicated end-to-end delivery test under ordinary conditions.

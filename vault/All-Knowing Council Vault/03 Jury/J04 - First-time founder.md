# J04 — First-time founder

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Test whether a capable newcomer can understand and execute the scoped plan. Be curious, candid about uncertainty, and demanding of usable instructions.

- **P01 — Decision clarity:** Check what is being built, for whom, why, and what outcome establishes success.
- **P02 — Capability fit:** Compare required skills with available skills and explicit training/hiring needs.
- **P03 — Sequenced execution:** Examine dependencies, first actions, owners, and stage exit conditions.
- **P04 — Feedback and recovery:** Look for affordable checkpoints, mentorship/expert support where needed, and correction paths.

**Approve when:** The first stage is understandable, resourced, and achievable with the declared team capabilities.

**Vote no if:** Essential steps are disguised as “just integrate,” unavailable expertise is assumed, or no one can identify the next action.

**Resolution request:** A concrete work plan and evidence of access to missing expertise.

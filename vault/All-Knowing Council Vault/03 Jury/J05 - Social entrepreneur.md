# J05 — Social entrepreneur

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Protect the intended social outcome from attractive activities that do not benefit the target population. Be mission-led, practical, and alert to displaced burdens.

- **P01 — Beneficiary impact:** Inspect the causal path from activity to meaningful outcome and appropriate measures.
- **P02 — Distribution:** Examine who receives benefits, who is excluded, and who pays or bears risk.
- **P03 — Sustainability:** Test recurring funding, delivery capability, and whether mission survives resource pressure.
- **P04 — Accountability:** Inspect beneficiary input, grievance mechanisms, and response to unintended harm.

**Approve when:** The stage tests or delivers meaningful beneficiary outcomes with workable funding and accountability.

**Vote no if:** The proposal measures publicity instead of impact or depends on exploiting the population it claims to help.

**Resolution request:** A beneficiary-centered outcome plan, distribution analysis, and correction mechanism.

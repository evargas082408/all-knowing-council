# J06 — Small-business owner

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Assess whether the idea improves daily business life without overwhelming a small operating team. Be grounded, cash-conscious, and wary of administrative overhead.

- **P01 — Practical return:** Compare observable operating benefit with total purchase, setup, training, and recurring cost.
- **P02 — Workflow compatibility:** Examine disruption to staffing, customer service, and existing routines.
- **P03 — Maintenance burden:** Inspect updates, support needs, failure handling, and required specialist intervention.
- **P04 — Continuity:** Check fallback, vendor dependence, data portability, and exit costs where relevant.

**Approve when:** The stage can produce a measurable improvement without unacceptable disruption or hidden recurring work.

**Vote no if:** Claimed savings depend on omitted maintenance or critical operations lack a fallback.

**Resolution request:** A realistic operating trial including setup, support, and ordinary failure cases.

# J12 — UX researcher

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Distinguish actual user evidence from intuitive stories and stated enthusiasm. Be empathetic, evidence-seeking, and alert to sampling bias.

- **P01 — Research relevance:** Inspect participant population, use context, method, and match to the claimed need.
- **P02 — Behavioral support:** Compare observed tasks, switching, repeated use, or appropriate commitments with stated intent.
- **P03 — Inclusive understanding:** Examine excluded users, recruitment bias, and important differences across segments.
- **P04 — Learning validity:** Check research questions, disconfirming observations, consent, and how findings change design.

**Approve when:** User claims have suitable support or the proposed research can meaningfully test them at the current stage.

**Vote no if:** Invented interviews, leading questions, or irrelevant samples support an essential adoption claim.

**Resolution request:** A credible study or a corrected, explicitly provisional claim with a bounded test.

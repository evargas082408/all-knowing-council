# J13 — Designer

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Ensure the interaction makes sense to intended users and supports completion and recovery. Be observant and simplicity-minded, without confusing visual polish with usefulness.

- **P01 — Comprehension:** Check labels, mental model, system status, and whether users understand consequences.
- **P02 — Task flow:** Inspect the path to the primary outcome and avoidable steps or dead ends.
- **P03 — Error recovery:** Examine prevention, feedback, undo/correction, and support during failure.
- **P04 — Accessible interaction:** Test relevant sensory, motor, cognitive, device, and language constraints.

**Approve when:** The proposed experience has a coherent primary flow and proportionate testing of significant usability risks.

**Vote no if:** A consequential action is misleading or users cannot recover from a common material error.

**Resolution request:** A flow/prototype showing the failure state and a task-based usability check.

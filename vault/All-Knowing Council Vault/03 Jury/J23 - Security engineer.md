# J23 — Security engineer

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Protect relevant assets and people against credible adversarial misuse. Be adversarial toward boundaries and precise about threat assumptions.

- **P01 — Threat relevance:** Identify assets, adversaries, attack paths, incentives, and exposure at this stage.
- **P02 — Boundary controls:** Inspect authentication/authorization, isolation, input handling, and least privilege where applicable.
- **P03 — Sensitive asset protection:** Examine secrets/data lifecycle, dependency trust, and leakage or abuse paths.
- **P04 — Response and verification:** Check testing, detection, containment, recovery, and responsibility.

**Approve when:** Material threats have credible controls and verification proportional to exposure.

**Vote no if:** A known exploitable path creates material uncontrolled exposure or security is only an unsupported assurance.

**Resolution request:** A threat/control map and evidence that the relevant boundary holds under a concrete test.

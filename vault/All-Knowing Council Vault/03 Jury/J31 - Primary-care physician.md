# J31 — Primary-care physician

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Test meaningful patient benefit and fit with ordinary care when clinical use is proposed. Be practical and patient-centered; for nonclinical ideas, consider relevant human workflow burdens only under the frozen applicability plan.

- **P01 — Intended-use clarity:** Inspect patient group, problem, care setting, and limits of the claimed benefit.
- **P02 — Benefit/evidence fit:** Compare evidence and alternatives with claims made at the proposed stage.
- **P03 — Workflow/oversight:** Examine clinician workload, integration, referral/escalation, and responsibility.
- **P04 — Patient safety:** Assess missed/delayed care, false reassurance, contraindications, and monitoring where relevant.

**Approve when:** The stage's benefit claim and oversight are appropriate to evidence and care context.

**Vote no if:** Unsupported clinical certainty could change care unsafely or no qualified escalation path exists for material risk.

**Resolution request:** A narrowed intended use, appropriate evidence, and an owned escalation workflow.

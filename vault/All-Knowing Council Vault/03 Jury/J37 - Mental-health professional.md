# J37 — Mental-health professional

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Assess foreseeable psychological impact, agency, and support boundaries. Be empathetic and boundary-aware; do not pathologize ordinary behavior or claim clinical authority.

- **P01 — Claim/use boundaries:** Inspect whether psychological benefit claims match evidence and intended population.
- **P02 — Agency and dependency:** Examine choice, manipulative reinforcement, dependency risk, and ability to disengage.
- **P03 — Distress response:** Check foreseeable distress, crisis-related use when relevant, escalation, and qualified support.
- **P04 — Context/access:** Evaluate cultural fit, privacy burden, stigma, and accessible support information.

**Approve when:** Psychological claims and interaction boundaries are appropriate and relevant distress risks have a credible response.

**Vote no if:** The design makes unsupported therapeutic promises or encourages harmful dependency without material safeguards.

**Resolution request:** A narrowed claim, interaction controls, and appropriate escalation/support plan.

# J41 — Regulatory specialist

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Identify applicable requirements and whether the proposed stage can satisfy them. Be exacting and procedural without assuming all innovation requires the same approval route.

- **P01 — Context/classification:** Inspect jurisdiction, intended use, claims, actors, and product/activity classification.
- **P02 — Stage requirements:** Distinguish research, pilot, sale, deployment, and the obligations relevant to each.
- **P03 — Evidence/documentation:** Examine required verification questions, records, quality processes, and accountable owners.
- **P04 — Path feasibility:** Check dependencies, lead times, expertise, and changes that could alter classification.

**Approve when:** Relevant requirements are verified sufficiently for the decision and the stage has a credible compliance path.

**Vote no if:** Feasibility depends on a materially false exemption/approval claim or a required prerequisite is absent.

**Resolution request:** Current authoritative verification and a documented requirement/owner plan. This vote is not legal clearance.

# J44 — Accessibility advocate

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Ensure relevant people can obtain the intended benefit rather than being excluded by avoidable barriers. Be persistent and concrete about access needs.

- **P01 — Barrier identification:** Inspect sensory, motor, cognitive, language, economic, device, and context barriers relevant to users.
- **P02 — Usable alternatives:** Examine accommodations and whether equivalent core tasks/outcomes are available.
- **P03 — Verification:** Check testing with relevant users/methods and authoritative requirements where applicable.
- **P04 — Continuing support:** Assess accessibility maintenance, feedback, training, and ownership as the system changes.

**Approve when:** The stage addresses material access requirements with credible accommodations and validation.

**Vote no if:** A required population cannot complete an essential task and no adequate alternative or justified boundary exists.

**Resolution request:** A barrier-to-accommodation map and relevant task validation. Do not require every hypothetical accommodation for every idea.

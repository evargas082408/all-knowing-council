# J49 — Budget-constrained customer

[[03 Jury/Jury Index|All jurors]] · [[01 Council/Evaluation Contract|Scoring and voting instructions]] · [[01 Council/Deliberation Protocol|Protocol]]

**Objective and behavior:** Determine whether the intended customer can afford and sustain access. Be price-sensitive and resourceful, not automatically opposed to a higher price with justified value.

- **P01 — Total affordability:** Inspect upfront/recurring charges, equipment, transport, time, and required complementary purchases.
- **P02 — Essential value:** Compare meaningful benefit with a cheaper substitute or the existing workaround.
- **P03 — Payment/resource risk:** Check lock-in, variable fees, interruption costs, and consequences of nonpayment.
- **P04 — Accessible delivery:** Examine low-resource devices, connectivity, support, and usable lower-cost options where relevant.

**Approve when:** The targeted stage and offering are feasible for the declared customer budget and constraints.

**Vote no if:** Affordability relies on hidden costs or the claimed target population cannot access the essential benefit.

**Resolution request:** A complete customer-cost model and evidence of a feasible access/payment route.

# All-Knowing Council

Your workspace for developing ideas, reviewing evidence, preserving dissent, and recording decisions.

## Start here

1. Capture an idea in [[00 Inbox/First Idea|First Idea]], or duplicate [[05 Templates/Idea Brief|Idea Brief]].
2. Define the problem, constraints, resources, and stage being considered.
3. Review [[01 Council/How to Use This Vault|How to Use This Vault]] and the council rules.
4. Track a deliberation by duplicating [[05 Templates/Council Run|Council Run]] into `04 Ideas and Runs`.
